# Citadel Edge – Services & Industries

## About Citadel Edge

Citadel Edge is an Australian-owned specialist technology company with over 25 years of experience, 250+ experts, and 5 offices across Asia-Pacific. They serve government, defence, health, higher education and commercial sectors, delivering secure, end-to-end software, cyber and technology solutions.

## Services (What They Deliver)

### Defence & National Security Systems
Advanced C4I, multi-domain data fusion, UAS/SATCOM integration, open systems architecture, deployable C2 tactical systems with edge computing, and combat system innovation (including their EdgeStack product). They have over two decades of trusted delivery to the ADF.

### Cyber Assurance
A virtual Chief Information Security Officer (vCISO) service providing strategic cyber oversight, compliance guidance and governance for boards and executives.

### Enterprise Software
Design, build and support of enterprise-grade software systems using agile methodologies, covering system monitoring, cloud support, performance tuning, security updates and feature enhancements.

### Audiovisual Collaboration
Design, delivery and support of AV collaboration platforms for command centres, auditoria, boardrooms, hearing rooms and hybrid workspaces. Includes their "AV EDGE" Partner Plans offering 24/7 service desk support. They have secure hubs in Canberra, Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane and Adelaide.

### Information Management & Governance
Delivered through their brand Kapish, built around OpenText Content Manager, with integrations via Microsoft Azure (Gold) and OpenText (Platinum) partnerships.

### Business Transformation Management
Also via Kapish, leveraging SAP (premier partner), IBM (preferred partner for Technology Business Management), and platforms like LeanIX, Signavio, Apptio, WalkMe and Cloudability.

### Data Management
Enterprise-grade data governance, data lineage, data pipelines, Master Data Management (MDM), data source integration, and advanced reporting and analytics using AI-enabled, scalable platforms.

### Workforce
Specialist staffing and project workforce services, placing skilled professionals across defence, government and data-critical industries.

## Industries Served

### Defence & National Security
ADF and national security community, delivering modernised legacy systems, edge-ready solutions, end-to-end cyber assurance and intelligence-led security.

### Government
Federal, state and local government agencies in Australia and New Zealand, including public sector healthcare agencies operating in uniquely complex environments.

### Health
Leading medical facilities such as the Royal Adelaide and Victorian Heart Hospitals; services include AV, video conferencing, enterprise software development and legacy system modernisation.

### Higher Education
Universities including Monash, University of Melbourne, Deakin and La Trobe; AV collaboration technologies, managed services, and cutting-edge learning spaces and lecture theatres.

### Commercial
Large enterprises including Coles, SEEK, Bank of Queensland and Virgin Australia; services include information management, enterprise architecture, cyber security and unified communications.
