# Citadel Edge – Tender Triage Process

## Overview

This document outlines the process for scoring and triaging incoming tenders against Citadel Edge's capability profile. It uses two reference files as the baseline:

- **`citadel-edge-services-industries.md`** — The capability and industry profile
- **`citadel-edge-case-studies.md`** — The evidence layer of proven delivery

The goal is to quickly determine fit, priority and likely win probability before committing bid resources.

---

## Step 1 — Extract the Tender's Key Dimensions

From the tender document, pull out the following critical fields:

- **Industry / Sector** — Who is the client and what sector do they operate in?
- **Required Capabilities** — What services or deliverables are being requested?
- **Contract Value** — What is the estimated value and duration?
- **Location** — Where will work be performed? Is it on-site, remote or classified?
- **Security Classification** — Are there clearance or sovereignty requirements?
- **Mandatory Criteria** — e.g. Australian ownership, panel membership, ISO certifications, indigenous procurement requirements
- **Timeline** — Submission deadline and expected contract start date

---

## Step 2 — Score Against the Services File

Use `citadel-edge-services-industries.md` to check capability alignment.

For each service the tender requires, ask:
- Does Citadel Edge offer this capability?
- At what depth — is it a core service or peripheral?
- Does the tender's industry appear in the industries we serve?

**Examples of strong alignment:**
- Tender requires AV Collaboration + Cyber Assurance → direct match
- Tender is from a federal government agency → direct match
- Tender requires sovereign Australian provider → direct match

**Examples of weak or no alignment:**
- Tender requires IoT hardware manufacturing → no match
- Tender is from the mining or resources sector → no match

---

## Step 3 — Score Against the Case Studies File

Use `citadel-edge-case-studies.md` as the evidence layer.

It is not enough to offer a service — you need proof of prior delivery. For each required capability in the tender, identify matching case studies and note:
- How directly relevant is the case study to this tender?
- Is the client type similar (e.g. federal government, defence, university)?
- Can this case study be referenced in the tender response?

**Example mappings:**
- Defence tender requiring secure video conferencing → DSVE, RF8000 F-35
- Government tender requiring records management → CSIRO, AusTender, NSW Dept of Education
- Commercial enterprise architecture tender → Coles (LeanIX), Officeworks (LeanIX), Blackmores (LeanIX)
- Health sector AV tender → Victorian Heart Hospital
- Navy workforce tender → Seaworthiness Assessment Services, Mechanical Engineering Capability

---

## Step 4 — Apply the Scoring Rubric

Score the tender across four dimensions for a total out of 100.

### Industry Match (0–20 points)
Does the tender's sector appear in Citadel Edge's industries?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 16–20 | Sector is a primary industry (Defence, Government, Health, Higher Education) |
| 8–15  | Sector is adjacent or partially served (e.g. large commercial enterprise) |
| 0–7   | Sector is outside Citadel Edge's served industries |

### Capability Match (0–30 points)
How many of the required services map directly to Citadel Edge offerings?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 24–30 | All or most required capabilities are core Citadel Edge services |
| 12–23 | Some capabilities match; others are gaps or stretch |
| 0–11  | Few or no capabilities align with what Citadel Edge delivers |

### Case Study Evidence (0–30 points)
How many directly relevant case studies exist to support the bid?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 24–30 | 3+ directly relevant case studies with similar client type and capability |
| 12–23 | 1–2 relevant case studies, or relevance is partial |
| 0–11  | No directly relevant case studies; evidence is a stretch |

### Differentiators (0–20 points)
Does Citadel Edge hold specific advantages that strengthen the bid?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 16–20 | Multiple differentiators apply (e.g. sovereign ownership + partner credentials + security clearance ready + 25+ years experience) |
| 8–15  | Some differentiators apply |
| 0–7   | Few or no differentiators are relevant to this tender |

**Key differentiators to consider:**
- Australian-owned sovereign provider
- 25+ years of experience in the relevant domain
- Security clearance capability
- SAP Premier Partner (LeanIX, Signavio)
- OpenText Platinum Partner
- IBM preferred Technology Business Management partner
- Existing panel or standing offer arrangements
- Veteran-led defence team

---

## Step 5 — Triage Decision

Based on the total score, assign a bid band:

| Band | Score | Decision |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Pursue** | 75–100 | Strong fit and clear evidence. Commit full bid resources and respond. |
| **Qualify** | 50–74 | Partial fit. Investigate capability gaps and client relationship before committing. |
| **No Bid** | 0–49 | Weak alignment. Cost of response likely exceeds win probability. Pass or monitor. |

---

## Step 6 — Bid Preparation Inputs

If the decision is **Pursue** or **Qualify**, use the triage outputs to inform the bid:

- **Recommended case studies to include** — from Step 3 mapping
- **Capability gaps to address** — subcontractors, partners or caveats needed
- **Key differentiators to lead with** — from Step 4
- **Risks to flag** — security classification, timeline, mandatory criteria gaps
