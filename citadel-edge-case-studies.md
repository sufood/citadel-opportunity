# Citadel Edge – Case Studies

## Overview

Citadel Edge brings together a unique blend of capabilities to deliver tailored solutions across multiple sectors. The following case studies showcase the breadth and impact of their work across defence, government, health, higher education and commercial industries.

---

### 1. Kapish delivers for the Yoorrook Justice Commission
**Capability:** Cyber Assurance / Information Management & Governance

Kapish was selected through open market procurement to deliver IT as a Service (ITaaS) to support this high-profile justice commission operating in a complex and high-accountability environment.

---

### 2. Victorian Heart Hospital
**Capability:** Audiovisual Collaboration

Citadel Edge led the Audiovisual work package for the Victorian Heart Hospital (VHH), Australia's first dedicated heart hospital, delivering world-class AV and collaboration technology across the facility.

---

### 3. Kapish and SAP LeanIX enables long-term technology view for Coles
**Capability:** Business Transformation Management

A change in leadership and strategy required Coles — one of Australia's largest retailers — to significantly overhaul its approach to enterprise architecture. Kapish implemented SAP LeanIX to provide long-term technology visibility and governance.

---

### 4. Department of Communities and Justice (DCJ) – NSW Supreme Court Banco
**Capability:** Audiovisual Collaboration

Citadel was awarded the most complex and highest-profile court AV project in NSW, selected from a prequalified procurement panel of AV integrators, to deliver AV systems for the NSW Supreme Court Banco courtroom.

---

### 5. Kapish takes Government's search cloud to the top of the class
**Capability:** Information Management & Governance

The NSW Department of Education sought to implement a cognitive search solution that would improve searching capabilities for staff, students, parents and the broader community. Kapish delivered a cloud-based solution to meet this need.

---

### 6. AusTender – Application Development
**Capability:** Data Management / Enterprise Software

Citadel Edge works with the Department of Finance to maintain AusTender — the federal government's procurement and tender platform — and to plan and implement significant system changes and refinements to meet evolving business needs.

---

### 7. CSIRO's migration to Kapish Content Manager Cloud
**Capability:** Information Management & Governance

CSIRO faced strategic and operational challenges in modernising their records management. Kapish migrated them to its cloud-hosted Content Manager platform, improving compliance, security and efficiency.

---

### 8. How Blackmores gained clarity, security and efficiency with Kapish and LeanIX
**Capability:** Business Transformation Management

With a complex IT infrastructure and a commitment to operational efficiency, Blackmores engaged Kapish to better manage their data and systems using LeanIX, resulting in greater clarity, improved security and streamlined operations.

### 9. University of Melbourne (UoM)

In 2019, the University of Melbourne engaged Citadel Edge to deliver AV Collaboration Services for staff meeting spaces, equipping modern and flexible collaboration environments across the campus.

### 11. Kapish delivers Commission in a Box

In an era of increasing scrutiny and public accountability, the Yoorrook Justice Commission turned to Kapish for end-to-end ICT and information governance — a comprehensive, rapidly deployable solution for standing up a commission's digital infrastructure.
